What is a transit?

The planets keep moving after you are born. A transit happens when a planet in today's sky makes an aspect (an angle) to a planet or point in your birth chart. That wakes up the part of you, and the area of life, that natal planet stands for.

  • Long-term transits: the chaptersJupiter, Saturn, Uranus, Neptune and Pluto stay on one point for months, so they bring the big themes of a year.
  • Short-term transits: the daysThe Sun, Mercury, Venus and Mars pass in days or weeks, bringing moods, conversations and events. The Moon changes sign every two to three days and colours your mood.
  • Applying and separatingApplying means the transit is still building towards exact. Separating means it has peaked and is fading.
  • The whole chartTwo Fishes also shows which of your houses the transiting planet is moving through, which houses the touched planet rules, and which other planets it is tied to, so you see the full story.
